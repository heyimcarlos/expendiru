# Spend accessibility

The fixture page keeps a skip link, a polite status region, and mobile controls at least 44px tall.

## Sub-features

- `skip` is the link Skip to spending.
- `status` is `#spend-status` with `aria-live="polite"`.
- `targets` are the chart mode buttons, next page, and the person select.
- `keys` move from Line to Bar with ArrowLeft.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the fixture site at 390px wide.
- Tab to Skip to spending, or focus a chart mode button and press an arrow key.

## Driving it with verify-expendiru

Preconditions:

- Doctor reports the fixture server at `http://127.0.0.1:4173`.
- The unfiltered count is on screen.

- **Read the controls.** Run `node scripts/verify-expendiru.mjs drive a11y`. The skip link text is `Skip to spending`. `#spend-status` is `aria-live="polite"` and `aria-atomic="true"`.
- **Measure targets.** `#spend-chart-pie`, `#spend-chart-bar`, `#spend-chart-line`, `#spend-page-next`, and `#spend-person` are at least 44px in both axes.
- **Press ArrowLeft.** Line is pressed before the key. Bar is pressed after it.
- **Proof.** `artifacts/verify-expendiru/a11y/mobile.png` and `result.json`. The page is not wider than the viewport.

## Gotchas

- The skip link sits off-screen until focused. The drive reads its text.
- The status sentence is visually clipped. Assert the attribute.
- Desktop buttons stay shorter than 44px. This drive is the 390px viewport.
